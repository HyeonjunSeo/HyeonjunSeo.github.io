4. Conclusion

The Naive Bayes classifier achieved an accuracy of 81.54%, showing that it can classify most reviews correctly.

The error analysis reveals several limitations:

  • Very short reviews provide insufficient information.
  • Negation and sentence context are difficult to interpret.
  • Mixed positive and negative expressions can cause confusion.
  • Spelling errors and unusual vocabulary can affect word-based predictions.

These errors highlight a limitation of Naive Bayes: it treats words largely independently and does not fully understand the context or meaning of a sentence.
